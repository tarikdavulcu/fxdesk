Support

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Need help with FXDesk? Write to fxdesk@tarikdavulcu.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and FXDesk version (Settings › About).

Restore your purchase

Open Settings › Purchase › Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy FXDesk.

Apple Watch shows an old snapshot

Open FXDesk on the iPhone. iOS delivers the snapshot when the watch is reachable; the watch always shows the snapshot’s age.

Results look unexpected

Check the quote direction (quote currency per 1 base currency), that bid is the price at which the desk buys the base currency, and whether quotes are stale.

Stored data could not be read

FXDesk keeps a backup copy of an unreadable data file and starts fresh. Contact support if you need help recovering it.
