Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Summary

FXDesk has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your desk profiles, quotes, calculation history, counter ledger transactions and settings are stored only on your iPhone. A compact snapshot of the active desk is shared with FXDesk’s own widgets and, through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, with the FXDesk app on your paired Apple Watch.

Free-calculation counter

To apply the three free calculations fairly, FXDesk stores identifiers of completed calculations in the iOS Keychain on this device. Keychain items can remain after the app is deleted. They contain no personal data and never leave the device.

Network connections

FXDesk works offline. If you enable the optional ECB reference rates and tap Refresh, the app downloads a public file from the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu); like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the ECB. If you tap “New Desk from TCMB Rates”, the app downloads the public daily rate file of the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (tcmb.gov.tr) in the same way. No quotes, calculations, transactions or identifiers are sent. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Sharing and export

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share a calculation summary or export a desk profile.

Deleting data

Delete history or profiles in the app, or delete the app to remove its stored data (the Keychain counter described above may remain).

Children

FXDesk is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page. Questions: fxdesk@tarikdavulcu.com.
